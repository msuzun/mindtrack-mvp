# MindTrack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 August 2026

MindTrack is an offline personal development tracking application.

## Data collection
MindTrack 1.0.0 does not require an account and does not intentionally collect,
transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

## Local data
Tasks, completion status, dates and progress information are stored locally on the
user's device using SQLite. This information is used only to provide the app's
tracking functionality.

## Analytics and advertising
MindTrack 1.0.0 does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or behavioral
tracking SDKs.

## Cloud services
MindTrack 1.0.0 does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization.

## Data deletion
Users can delete locally stored app data by clearing the application's storage or
uninstalling MindTrack, subject to the device operating system's behavior and any
device-level backups configured by the user.

## Children
MindTrack is not specifically directed to children.

## Health disclaimer
MindTrack is not a medical, psychological, diagnostic, or treatment service.
Cognitive exercises do not guarantee an increase in an IQ score.

## Changes
If future versions add accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or other data
processing, this policy will be updated before those features are released.
